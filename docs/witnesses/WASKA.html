Comment on passage THSMV01P032A: 1

1 This fragment is not found in HSNX. There are a complex of traditions that revolve around ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭālib and his performance of wuḍūʿ. Although they most likely deal with the same event since ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbbās is the witness for each, they are sufficiently different to treat as distinct traditions. The fragment referred to here is the “entering” tradition, where ʿAlī enters the home of ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿAbbās and asks for water for wuḍūʿ. While it refers to “wiping” it is placed in a specific context and will be categorized here as the “entering” tradition. This tradition is found in WYWAT (Yaḥyā b. Wāḍiḥ al-Anṣārī Abū Tumaylah), WASKA (ʿAbdah (ʿAbd al-Raḥmān) b. Sulaymān al-Kilābī al-Kūfī), WMSAH (Muḥammad b. Salamah b. ʿAbd Allāh al-Bāhilī al-Ḥarrānī), WISUL (Ismāʿīl b. ʿUlayah), and WISIB (Ismāʿīl b. Ibrāhīm b. Muḥammad b. Ṭalḥah al-Ḥijāzī), WARMZ (ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Muḥammad b. Ziyād al-Muḥāribī al-Kūfī).
